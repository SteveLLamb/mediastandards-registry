[ACTIVE]

Bringing Stage Events with Real Actors to Your Home in VR: A Low-Complexity Solution for Immersive Media Streaming

Metadata

Publisher
SMPTE
Doc Type
Journal Article
Content Type
Original Research
Abbreviated Title
SMPTE Motion Imaging Jour.
Volume
134, No. 6, pp. 59–68
Abstract
In this paper, we present a novel method for integrating the stereoscopic content of a live act into a virtual reality (VR) environment. This opens a new level of immersion when watching the recording of a stage event using a head-mounted display. In contrast to existing solutions, we offer proper depth perception, allow for a low complexity recording and distribution, and still avoid any motion sickness caused by objects being close to the observer. To this end, we separate the digitization of the event location from the recording of the actor's performance. As a main contribution of the paper, we show how to achieve high visual quality when compositing the stereo recording of the actors with the reconstructed 3D model of the event location. To achieve this, we have developed a tool that enables simulating the 3D perception of stereo video, depending on the selected stereo parameters, such as inter-camera distance and focal length. By these means, we can ensure a seamless transition between the stereo video and the 3D model of the event location, resulting in superior visual quality. Numerous lessons learned conclude our paper, demonstrating how we overcame the practical problems encountered while recording a real theater play.
