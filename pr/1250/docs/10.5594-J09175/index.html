Bibliography on Holograms—III

Abstract
Bibliography on Holograms—I (Journal, April 7966) and II (August 1966), compiled by R. P. Chambers and J. S. Courtney-Pratt, Bell Telephone Laboratories, Inc., Murray Hill, N. J. 07971, included some 320 titles and abstracts of papers on holograms. The present Bibliography, contributed by R. P. Chambers and B. A. Stevens (also of Bell Telephone Laboratories), includes more than 100 titles. Authors, titles of papers and sources are listed alphabetically by the first author. With only two or three exceptions, the papers in the present Bibliography were published during 1966. The two earlier Bibliographies covered the years 1948 through 1965 and the first four months of 1966. In view of the rapidly growing body of literature dealing with holograms and related techniques, these contributions are of special and continuing value.—Ed.
